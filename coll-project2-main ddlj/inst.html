Voting Instructions

  • Login First: Enter your registered username and password to access the voting page.
  • Go to Voting Section: Click on the “Vote Now” button from the dashboard.
  • Select Your Candidate: Choose one candidate carefully from the list.
  • Confirm Your Selection: Review your selected candidate before submitting.
  • Submit Your Vote: Click on the “Submit Vote” button.
  • Vote Successfully Submitted: Once submitted, your vote cannot be changed.
